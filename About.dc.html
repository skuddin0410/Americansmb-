Why we exist

The rules are written for everyone. We explain them for small businesses.

Small businesses are the backbone of the American economy, yet most of the tax rules, funding programs and market changes that affect them are written in dense legal language for large companies with in-house experts.

We close that gap. Our editors follow federal and state announcements every day and ask one question of every story: what does this mean for your business, and what should you do next? The answer is always in plain language, with the official sources linked so you can check them yourself.

99%

of all U.S. businesses are small businesses — more than 36 million of them. They are who we write for.
