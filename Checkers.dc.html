Two questions owners ask us most: does my business count as “small” for SBA programs, and do I need to send a contractor a 1099-NEC?

Eligibility

Am I a “small business”?

The SBA sets a size limit for each industry. Meeting it is required for SBA loans and small-business set-aside contracts.

Result{{szTitle}}{{szText}}

Receipts are generally your average annual receipts over the last five completed fiscal years; employees are your average over the last 24 months. Count affiliated businesses too. Standards from the SBA table of size standards (13 CFR 121.201); a 2026 proposal would change some limits. Confirm with the SBA’s size standards tool before you bid or apply. Contractors

Do I need to file a 1099-NEC?

For payments you made to a contractor or freelancer in 2026 (forms due February 1, 2027).
